AlphaNIST in ROS 2

A reimplementation of AlphaNIST: Emergent Assembly Skills from Binary Task Rewards (Zhang et al., arXiv:2610.13354). One PPO policy learns to assemble NIST Task Board #1 parts with a Franka Panda, using only a binary "fully assembled" reward.

The policy in action

Each episode shows the task, how it started, the assembly error, and ends on a green ASSEMBLED or red NOT ASSEMBLED banner.

02 · Method

What the paper asks for

  • A binary reward: the policy only gets credit for a fully assembled part.
  • A reset bank of start states, so the policy practises from partial assemblies as well as from scratch.
  • Success-guided beta sampling to choose which start states to train on.
03 · Scope

Four-task subset

The reimplementation covers four tasks: Ø8 and Ø12 pegs, a medium gear, and an M16 nut on a bolt. The mapping from paper to code, and every deviation from the paper, is documented in the repository.

The robot is Franka's official model (franka_description 2.9.0). Training runs in a scaled-down MuJoCo setup; ROS 2 and Gazebo run inside Docker.
