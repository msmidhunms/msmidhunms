01 · Overview

Four disciplines, one platform

Businesses usually run search, social, ads and reviews in separate tools, with separate data and a lot of manual writing and checking. This platform brings them together. My team designs and builds the AI features across all four modules as the business needs them, on a shared AI layer that knows each client's brand and data.

04 · Shared AI layer

Built once, used everywhere

Context

Retrieval over client data (RAG)

Client information is stored in a vector database and retrieved at generation time, so outputs are grounded in each client's own business rather than generic text.

Consistency

Brand voice and guidelines

Each client's tone and rules are applied to everything the AI writes, from blog posts and captions to ad copy and review replies.

Planning

AI content calendar

Plans articles and posts over time, tying SEO and social output into one schedule.

Models

Hosted LLM APIs

Generation and analysis run on hosted large language models, behind one layer that every module calls.
